About Incrife

Incrife (Angel Córdoba El Hannouti) is a Spanish urban artist, music producer and music video director born in Barcelona on 28 September 2006. Of Spanish and Italian descent, he grew up in the La Mina neighbourhood, a place that is part of his identity and creative vision.

His project began in 2022 with the aim of uniting music and image within a single artistic universe. His sound blends influences from afro, tekno and afrotrap, building a style of his own where musical energy and visual identity carry equal weight.

In 2025 he released “The Death of the Flock” with Ouxxox, a project that marked a different stage in his career.

Beyond music, Incrife develops projects around image and creativity. In 2026 he founded Kurtusinc, a project focused on developing models and creating new opportunities in audiovisual work and fashion.

He stays closely connected to his surroundings, collaborating with and supporting people from his neighbourhood, La Mina, encouraging creativity and creating opportunities through his projects.

He continues to develop his artistic project, exploring new sounds and building a universe where music, visuals and community are part of one experience.

FAQ

Everything about Incrife

Incrife is a bouyon music artist with afro, tekno and pop influences. Quick answers for bouyon fans worldwide.

Who is Incrife?

Incrife is the stage name of Angel Córdoba El Hannouti, a Spanish music artist born in Barcelona on 28 September 2006 and raised in the La Mina neighbourhood. He makes bouyon, afro, tekno and pop, and produces and directs his own music videos.

Where is Incrife from?

Incrife was born in Barcelona and grew up in La Mina, in Sant Adrià de Besòs. His roots and urban surroundings shape his artistic identity.

What music does Incrife make?

Incrife makes urban music built around bouyon, blended with afro, tekno, afrotrap and pop. His sound pairs rhythmic energy with a strong visual identity.

What is bouyon?

Bouyon is a fast, danceable Caribbean music genre born on the island of Dominica, also hugely popular in Guadeloupe, Martinique and France.

What is Incrife’s best-known music video?

The official video for LUCKY, released on VEVO and also available on Apple Music.

Where can I listen to Incrife’s music?

On Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, TIDAL, Amazon Music, Qobuz and YouTube.

Which songs has Incrife released?

Incrife’s public songs are LUCKY, Crazy House, Noil, Dark Zone and The Death of the Flock (with Ouxxox). They are available on Spotify and all major platforms.

When is Incrife’s next concert or tour?

Tour dates will be announced soon on this website and on Incrife’s social media.

How can I contact Incrife for collaborations or bookings?

Email contact@incrife.com. Incrife welcomes collaboration and booking requests from any country.
